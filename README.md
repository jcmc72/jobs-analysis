## ✅ Interview Readiness Checklist

You're ready for an interview when:

- [ ] Can answer any STAR question in 90-120 seconds (no notes)
- [ ] Can explain your gap confidently in 60 seconds
- [ ] Deliver opening statement smoothly and naturally
- [ ] Ask 3-4 probing questions showing deep understanding
- [ ] Cite 3-4 specific stories from your resume proving capabilities
